# Quest for Everyday Heroes

> An immersive educational VR role-playing game developed as part of MSc research exploring how immersive technologies can support daily life skills in children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).

## Research Project

**Research Title:** *Exploring the Application of Immersive Technologies in Educational Games to Improve the Daily Life Skills for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder*

**Game Title:** *Quest for Everyday Heroes*

**Research Area:** Virtual Reality • Immersive Technologies • Educational Games • Autism Spectrum Disorder • Serious Games • Human-Computer Interaction

## About the Project

Quest for Everyday Heroes is an immersive educational VR role-playing game designed to provide children with Autism Spectrum Disorder with a safe and structured virtual environment in which they can practise everyday activities and social interactions.

The project explores the application of immersive technologies in educational games, with a particular focus on supporting the development and practice of daily life skills. Players progress through interactive scenarios inspired by real-world activities and complete tasks through exploration, decision-making, interaction with virtual objects, and engagement with non-player characters (NPCs).

The game is being developed as the practical component of my MSc in Computing by Research at Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU).

## Project Goals

- Create immersive VR scenarios based on everyday activities.
- Provide structured opportunities to practise daily life skills in a controlled virtual environment.
- Explore VR interaction design appropriate for educational and research-oriented experiences.
- Incorporate task-based gameplay, environmental interaction, and NPC-based social scenarios.
- Investigate how immersive educational games can complement existing approaches to learning and skills practice.

## Gameplay & Learning Experience

The experience is structured around everyday routines and activities. Depending on the scenario, the player may need to navigate an environment, interact with objects, communicate with NPCs, make choices, and complete a sequence of practical tasks.

Example systems and activities include:

- Daily routine and task-based scenarios
- Object interaction and spatial tasks
- NPC interactions and dialogue choices
- Shopping and real-world activity simulations
- Navigation and environmental exploration
- Educational mini-games and cognitive activities
- Task completion and player-performance tracking

## Development

**Engine:** Unity 6  
**Programming:** C#  
**XR Framework:** Unity XR Interaction Toolkit  
**Target Hardware:** Meta Quest 3 / VR  
**Rendering:** Universal Render Pipeline (URP)

The project combines gameplay programming, XR interaction design, environment development, NPC systems, task logic, UI/UX, data collection mechanisms, and performance considerations for standalone VR hardware.

## Research & Evaluation

The project forms part of an academic research study investigating immersive technologies and educational games for children with ASD. The research includes requirements gathering, iterative design and development, and evaluation of the VR experience.

Evaluation focuses on interaction with the designed scenarios and tasks, including measures such as task completion, attempts, engagement with activities, and interaction choices where appropriate to the study design.

## Repository Purpose

This repository is primarily a **research and development portfolio case study**. It documents the design, development process, technical systems, and selected implementation examples from Quest for Everyday Heroes.

The complete research application, participant data, confidential research materials, and any content that cannot be publicly distributed are intentionally excluded.

## Ethics & Privacy

This research involves a sensitive participant group. Participant privacy, informed consent, research ethics, and responsible handling of research data are therefore central to the project.

No personally identifiable participant information, confidential study data, recordings, or protected research materials will be published in this repository.
